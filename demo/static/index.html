About the model & its limits
  • Task: binary classifier on 9 markers (cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides, blood pressure, heart rate, BMI, CRP). The label means “not healthy” for any condition in the source blood-sample dataset, not cardiovascular disease specifically.
  • Training data: 12,837 rows: 2,837 from the original dataset plus 10,000 synthetic rows (TVAE). About 79% of rows are labelled “not healthy”, so a model that always said “flagged” would score ~79%.
  • Held-out results (20% split, 2,568 rows): 90.9% accuracy; for the “healthy” class precision 0.81 / recall 0.72, for “not healthy” 0.93 / 0.96. Healthy people are missed as flagged about 1 time in 4.
  • Reading the bars: values are contributions in log-odds. Right (orange) pushes toward “flagged”, left (teal) away from it. They are explanations of this model, not of biology.
  • Outside the training range (sliders span it) predictions are unreliable; the page warns you when that happens.
